# Vision

This is what we're building:
Thing that should exist but (i think?) doesn't:
- Shared company brain that you connect to any agent
- Nice Ul for viewing pages
- Permissions / suggest changes mode
- Versioning
- Works with company SSO / RBAC
- Also bundles connectors to tools

@RhysSullivan is doing the "bundle access to connectors" part of this with executor.sh
but i think connectors alone are not enough, the context/skill layer is crucial to bundle with it

And here are some relevant quotes from X:

Every company has critical know-how scattered across people's heads, old Slack threads, support tickets, and databases, and AI agents can't operate like that. We think every company in the world is going to need a new primitive: a living map of how the company works that turns its own artifacts into an executable skills file for AI.

company brain (context) + company ai stack (skills, hooks, workflows)

One of the best examples of actual Al change management succeeding is inserting a single brain into Slack/Teams that your company can work directly with.
Their daily workflows already involve chat.
Speaking with agents feels no different than working with humans.
